Program Overview

Data analysts bridge business problem-solving with technical data querying. This course is specifically structured for students and working professionals aiming to switch into high-growth tech careers without writing hundreds of lines of code.

Syllabus

4-Month Industry Curriculum

Module 1 · Weeks 1–3 Spreadsheets

Advanced Excel & Business Modeling

XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, Dynamic Arrays, Nested IFs, Pivot Tables, Power Query automation, What-If analysis, scenario manager, and financial KPI dashboards.

Module 2 · Weeks 4–8 Databases

SQL for Data Analytics & Querying

Relational database architecture, SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, HAVING, INNER/LEFT/RIGHT JOINs, subqueries, Common Table Expressions (CTEs), and Window Functions (RANK, DENSE_RANK, ROW_NUMBER, LAG/LEAD).

Module 3 · Weeks 9–13 Power BI

Microsoft Power BI & DAX Mastery

Connecting multiple data sources, data modeling, relationship cardinality, calculated columns vs measures, advanced DAX (CALCULATE, FILTER, ALL, RELATED, Time Intelligence), and publishing to Power BI Service.

Module 4 · Weeks 14–16 Visual Storytelling

Tableau & Executive Presentation

Building charts, geographic maps, dual-axis graphs, story points in Tableau, integrating Python for analytics, and presenting business recommendations to leadership.
