About KeepClicks®

Where Technology Education Meets Digital Excellence.

Our Story

KeepClicks was founded with a singular vision: to bridge the critical gap between traditional tech education and real-world industry demands. Started in 2022 in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, we realized that students were graduating without the practical skills necessary to thrive in a digital-first world.

Today, KeepClicks stands unique as both a premier IT Academy and a full-scale Digital Marketing Agency. We don't just teach technology; we practice it daily for our global clients, bringing live insights directly into our classrooms.
